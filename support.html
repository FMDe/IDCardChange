About the App

IDCardChange helps you change the background color of an ID photo. Photo processing is performed locally on your device.

Common Questions

  • If photo selection does not work, check whether photo library permission is enabled for the app in Settings.
  • If saving does not work, check whether the app has permission to add photos to your photo library.
  • If the background result is not ideal, try a photo with clear lighting and a simple original background.

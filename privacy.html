Privacy Policy

Updated: September 9, 2026

IDCardChange is an ID photo background editing app. This policy explains how the app handles photos, permissions, and local data.

Photo Processing

  • The app lets you select a photo, detect the person area, replace the background color, preview the result, and save the processed photo.
  • Photo processing happens locally on your device.
  • The app does not upload, sell, or share your photos.

Data Collection

The app does not collect personal data, does not use accounts, does not include advertising tracking, and does not include third-party analytics SDKs.

Photo Library Permissions

  • Photo library read access is used only so you can choose the photo you want to edit.
  • Photo library add access is used only to save the processed photo to your photo library.
  • If you deny a permission, the related choose or save feature may not be available.

Local Data

The app may store your language choice and privacy policy acceptance status locally on your device. This information is not sent to the developer.
